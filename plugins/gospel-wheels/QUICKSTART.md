# Gospel Wheels - Quick Start Guide

Get authentic alt-country Hammond organ in 30 seconds.

---

## Setup (30 seconds)

1. **Load M-Tron Pro IV** in your DAW
2. **Select Hammond organ tape** (B3 Mk1 recommended)
3. **Insert Gospel Wheels** before M-Tron Pro IV in MIDI chain:
   ```
   MIDI Track → Gospel Wheels → M-Tron Pro IV
   ```
4. **Play a chord** - Gospel Wheels will generate organ voicing automatically

Done! You're making organ music.

---

## 4 Ready-to-Use Presets

### 1. Sparse Atmospheric (Verse Organ)
```
Style: Atmospheric
Harmonics: 40%
Swell: 60%
Register: 55%
Auto-Thicken: OFF
```
**Use for:** Verses, quiet sections, vocal support  
**Character:** Spacious, subtle, doesn't compete with vocals

---

### 2. Full Sustained (Chorus Organ)
```
Style: Sustained
Harmonics: 70%
Swell: 40%
Register: 50%
Auto-Thicken: ON
```
**Use for:** Choruses, big sections, full arrangements  
**Character:** Rich, harmonic, classic Hammond sound

---

### 3. Rhythmic Comping (Groove Organ)
```
Style: Comping
Harmonics: 50%
Rhythm: 65%
Register: 52%
Tempo: 120 BPM (match your song)
Auto-Thicken: ON
```
**Use for:** Upbeat songs, creating groove, rhythmic interest  
**Character:** Percussive, rhythmic, adds movement

---

### 4. Lonesome Prairie (Ballad Organ)
```
Style: Swell
Harmonics: 60%
Swell: 75%
Voicing: 85%
Register: 48%
Auto-Thicken: ON
```
**Use for:** Ballads, emotional moments, dynamic builds  
**Character:** Swelling, breathing, expressive

## Quick Tips

### DO:
- ✅ Play simple triads - Gospel Wheels will enhance them
- ✅ Use Atmospheric for verses, Sustained for choruses
- ✅ Enable Auto-Thicken for classic Hammond sound
- ✅ Set Voicing to 70%+ for ballads (smooth transitions)
- ✅ Adjust Register to find sweet spot (usually 45-55%)

### DON'T:
- ❌ Set Harmonics to 100% (too muddy)
- ❌ Use Comping with Rhythm > 80% (too busy)
- ❌ Forget to match Tempo parameter to your song
- ❌ Expect prog-rock solos (this is for songwriting)

## Song Building (Verse → Chorus → Bridge)

### Verse (Sparse)
1. Set Style: **Atmospheric**
2. Set Harmonics: **35%**
3. Set Register: **55%** (slightly high)
4. Play simple triads or root-fifth dyads

### Chorus (Full)
1. Switch Style: **Sustained**
2. Increase Harmonics: **65%**
3. Center Register: **50%**
4. Enable Auto-Thicken: **ON**
5. Play fuller chords (triads, 7ths)

### Bridge (Dynamic)
1. Switch Style: **Swell**
2. Set Harmonics: **60%**
3. Increase Swell: **75%**
4. Set Voicing: **80%** (smooth)
5. Play sustained chords, let swells create movement

## Troubleshooting

**Q: Not hearing anything?**  
A: Check MIDI routing. Gospel Wheels → M-Tron Pro IV. Gospel Wheels doesn't make sound, only MIDI.

**Q: Sounds muddy?**  
A: Lower Harmonics to 40%. Turn off Auto-Thicken. Try Atmospheric style.

**Q: Not enough rhythm in Comping mode?**  
A: Match Tempo parameter to your song BPM. Increase Rhythm to 70%.

**Q: Chord changes sound jumpy?**  
A: Increase Voicing to 80% for smoother voice leading.

**Q: Want lower/higher organ?**  
A: Adjust Register parameter. 0% = low, 100% = high. Try 45-55% first.

## M-Tron Pro IV Recommended Settings

**Tape:** B3 Mk1 (classic) or C3 (darker)  
**Tone:** 55%  
**Tape Wear:** 15% (adds character)  
**Wow/Flutter:** 8% (subtle movement)  
**Reverb:** To taste (Gospel Wheels works great into reverb)
